# Claim Boundaries

## Safe Current Claim

Standard coarse-graining choices can preserve useful scalar rotation information while materially altering local electric-Weyl/eigenframe structure seen by relational witnesses.

## Safe Public Language

- Controlled weak-field curvature proxy study.
- Computational-methods result about information transfer under averaging.
- Evidence that averaging methods have curvature-side transfer functions worth studying.
- Internal convergence evidence, not a public physics claim by itself.

## Do Not Claim Yet

- This explains galaxy rotation curves.
- This is evidence for or against dark matter.
- This proves standard averaging is wrong.
- This is full-GR numerical relativity.
- This establishes cosmological backreaction in real survey data.
