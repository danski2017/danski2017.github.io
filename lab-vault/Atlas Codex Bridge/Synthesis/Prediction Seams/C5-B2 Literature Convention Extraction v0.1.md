## Literature Sources Reviewed

### Costa and Herdeiro
Target:
A gravito-electromagnetic analogy based on tidal tensors.

Use:
Primary tidal-tensor convention target.

Relevant extraction:
Costa and Herdeiro define electric and magnetic parts of the Weyl tensor relative to an observer U, with the magnetic part built from the dual Weyl tensor. They state that the electric and magnetic Weyl parts are symmetric and traceless.

Boundary:
They also emphasize observer dependence of the electric/magnetic Weyl split.

Atlas implication:
This supports the OBS_034/OBS_036 insistence that B_ij is observer/tetrad-dependent and must be declared before any sign-pattern work.

### Wylleman et al.
Target:
Poynting vector, super-Poynting vector, and principal observers in electromagnetism and general relativity.

Use:
Bel-Robinson / super-Poynting convention and claim-boundary target.

Relevant extraction:
Bel-Robinson superenergy and super-Poynting vector are formal analogues of electromagnetic energy density and Poynting vector. However, the source warns that these objects have unusual dimensions and unclear direct physical meaning. It also notes convention differences in the Bel-Robinson tensor normalization.

Atlas implication:
This supports the C5-B2 boundary: P_BR is a Weyl-superenergy directionality witness, not ordinary energy flux, not gravitational-wave luminosity, and not Bondi mass loss.
