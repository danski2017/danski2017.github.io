## 1. Purpose

Determine whether the Atlas finite-domain electric-Weyl square ledger belongs lawfully beside the Bel-Robinson/superenergy family before any binary derivation.

The gate exists to prevent `B_E(a)` from being derived as if it were already a radiative balance object. It asks whether the candidate ledger is same-family with curvature-square superenergy objects, and what must be declared before any comparison is lawful.

## 2. Objects to Compare

- Atlas ledger candidate: `B_E = integral E_ij E^ij dV`
- Bel-Robinson tensor / superenergy density
- Electric and magnetic Weyl decomposition: `E_ij`, `B_ij`
- Radiative Weyl channel / `Psi_4`
- News tensor / Bondi channel
- Isaacson effective stress-energy

## 3. Same-Family Test

Questions:

- Is `B_E` only the electric part of a fuller curvature-square object?
- Does a radiative comparison require `E_ij E^ij + B_ij B^ij`?
- Does omitting magnetic-Weyl terms make `B_E` near-zone/static only?
- What observer or slicing choice is required?

Preflight read:

`B_E` is same-family only with the electric part of curvature-square bookkeeping. It is not automatically the full Bel-Robinson/superenergy object because it omits magnetic-Weyl terms and does not yet declare an observer/tetrad, hypersurface, integration domain, or flux surface.

Radiative comparison likely requires a fuller electric-plus-magnetic Weyl object or an explicitly radiative scalar/channel such as `Psi_4` or News. Omitting magnetic-Weyl terms keeps the Atlas `B_E` branch safest as a static or near-zone diagnostic ledger.

## 4. Boundary Between Ledgers

Static exterior Weyl-electric budget:

- finite-source identity measure,
- electric-Weyl square content under declared source model,
- not ordinary energy,
- not a radiative flux.

Finite-domain near-zone curvature ledger:

- volume integral over a declared slice/domain,
- cutoff/worldtube dependent,
- potentially useful as a diagnostic of binary separation,
- not automatically wave-zone radiation.

Radiative curvature-square flux:

- wave-zone or asymptotic channel,
- likely needs `Psi_4`, News, or Bel-Robinson-like flux construction,
- requires observer/tetrad/slicing or extraction convention,
- not equivalent to `B_E` without mediation.

Ordinary energy flux:

- Bondi mass loss, Isaacson effective stress-energy, or quadrupole luminosity family,
- requires explicit dimensional conversion and assumptions,
- cannot be inferred from electric-Weyl square volume change alone.

## 5. Required Declarations Before B_E(a)

- integration domain,
- source exclusion/worldtube rule,
- slicing/gauge,
- observer/tetrad convention,
- whether magnetic Weyl is included,
- whether comparison target is superenergy or ordinary energy.

No `B_E(a)` derivation should be treated as radiative, conserved, or predictive unless these declarations are made and pass review.

## 6. Preliminary Ruling Options

Chosen ruling:

```text
C. Both branches should be carried separately: B_E(a) diagnostic branch and Bel-Robinson radiative comparison branch.
```

Rationale:

The Atlas `B_E` object remains useful as a finite-domain electric-Weyl diagnostic branch, especially in continuity with the finite-source budget language. But a radiative comparison should not be forced onto that object. The Bel-Robinson/superenergy family is the more lawful comparison target for curvature-square radiative work, provided observer/tetrad, hypersurface, magnetic-Weyl inclusion, and flux conventions are declared.
