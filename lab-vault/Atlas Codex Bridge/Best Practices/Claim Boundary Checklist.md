# Claim Boundary Checklist

Use before writing public-facing language or paper claims.

## Ask First

- Is this a simulation result, a mathematical result, or an interpretation?
- Is the scene toy, effective, observationally anchored, or data-driven?
- Is the field weak-field proxy, full GR, or another declared approximation?
- Are witness panels declared?
- Are metrics preregistered or post-hoc?
- Does the result persist under source-count and resolution ladders?
- Is there a known null or failure mode?

## Frontier Signal Checks

- Does this result strain GR itself, or only a workflow/convention?
- Have we escalated rigor before dismissing it?
- Have we preserved the signal with low claim status?
- Are we avoiding both overclaim and premature rejection?
- Is the claim ceiling explicitly declared?

## Safe Language

- "suggests"
- "in this controlled proxy scene"
- "method-dependent transfer function"
- "curvature-side information loss"
- "witness-panel dependent"
- "internal convergence evidence"

## Avoid Unless Earned

- "proves"
- "explains galaxy rotation curves"
- "evidence against dark matter"
- "evidence for dark energy/backreaction"
- "new gravitational physics"
- "full GR simulation"

## Required Non-Claims

For current B006-B014 family:

- no dark-matter claim
- no dark-energy claim
- no cosmological parameter claim
- no GR/SR challenge
- not full numerical relativity
- weak-field/effective-source limits declared
