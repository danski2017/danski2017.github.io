# GFRO Time-Slice Doctrine Re-Promotion 2026-05-19

## Summary

After recent modeling experiments and GPT lab-director discussion, the lab
reversed the short BCW default-method correction.

GFRO is re-promoted as lawful Atlas modeling doctrine for declared
same-time-slice scenes.

BCW is demoted to an unproven and unnecessary modeling method for current
static-slice work.

## Reason

Atlas static diagnostics are performed on a declared time slice.

If all sources sample the field at the same time slice as the observer/sampler,
then the collective source influence is already baked into the field sampled at
that moment.

That makes an additional barycentric compression witness unnecessary for
lawful static-slice diagnostic geometry.

## Restored Diagnostic Doctrine

The following diagnostic structures are lawful doctrine again when derived from
declared same-time-slice GFRO field sampling with ledger, validation, and claim
status:

- GCS
- faces
- seams
- nodes
- junctions
- zero sets
- parity-context supports
- Lumen Lattice / Released Coherence Lattice
- other declared GFRO diagnostic structures

## Boundary

This correction applies to same-time-slice modeling.

It does not certify future time-evolution, delayed-interaction,
history-dependent, or dynamic causal models. Those require separate scene
passports, ledgers, validation rules, and claim ceilings.

Compact rule:

> Same slice, same field, lawful diagnostic geometry.
