## Updated Abstract

We test how galaxy source-structure coarse representations transfer scalar rotation-like information relative to local electric-Weyl/eigenframe structure. Across local SPARC/HALOGAS source-proxy scenes and declared baryonic augmentations, sector-style coarse branches preserve scalar summaries and tensor/eigenframe structure with different fidelities. A ring-resolved residual closure test does not show that absolute electric-Weyl loss closes SPARC scalar residuals. However, after scalar-denominator auditing, the residual-adjacent `E/vc2` hierarchy survives support, smoothing, and inclination-style ledger perturbations in the three primary non-edge-on galaxies. The result is a bounded transfer-function and mechanism result, not a dark-matter explanation, MOND test, or survey-level law.
