# G001P Gaia Parity-Delta Overlay

G001P tests the Claude wing hypothesis that high retained-vs-coarse averaging deltas in the Gaia 25 pc scene should cluster near parity/handoff structure.

The discriminating null compares Atlas parity/handoff proxy margins against Hill-style and Voronoi boundary margins on the same witness set.

## Boundary Predictors

- `parity`: low top-two gravitational contribution margin.
- `hill`: low distance to a simplified nearest-pair Hill-style shell.
- `voronoi`: low nearest/second-nearest distance margin.

These are declared first-pass proxies, not final physical boundary definitions.

## Atlas Read

G001P does not validate parity-specific localization in this first-pass Gaia overlay. It does show that high-delta regions are spatially boundary-sensitive under the tested predictors.

The honest result is:

> The Gaia 25 pc averaging split is spatially structured, but the first-pass parity proxy does not outperform Hill-style and Voronoi boundary comparators.

That is a useful result. It means the discriminating null worked, and it prevents the lab from prematurely calling this external validation of the parity network.
