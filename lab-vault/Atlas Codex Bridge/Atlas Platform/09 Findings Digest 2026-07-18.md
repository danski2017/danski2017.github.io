entropy ("high-attribution-entropy tidal-amplitude troughs"; lab
shorthand: cancellation troughs). Between them, mid-amplitude bands

structure accretes and locks (finger-trap); under roster-internal
perturbation both the objects AND their carrier rosters are invariant;
under imposed exterior context, carriers reorganize before troughs
evaporate at a threshold ~ each trough's own floor depth.
