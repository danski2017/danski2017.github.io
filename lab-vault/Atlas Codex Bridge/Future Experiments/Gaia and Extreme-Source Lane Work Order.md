# Gaia and Extreme-Source Lane Work Order

This note preserves the next-up experiment lane after the B027-B037 galaxy coarse-graining review package.

Canonical proposal:

- `codex_context/proposals/Gaia_Extreme_Source_Lane_Work_Order.md`

## Scientific Question

Can the Atlas tensor/scalar transfer asymmetry survive contact with the largest available real stellar-source ledgers, beginning with a clean Gaia local stellar field and then adding structured binary and compact-object overlays?

## G001

Completed first-contact pilot:

- [[../Experiments/G001 Gaia Local Clean Stellar Ledger]]

The completed pilot used the existing Gaia 25 pc cache and a declared photometric mass proxy. The larger Lane 1 version should use Gaia DR3 local high-quality astrometry, with Gaia DR3 astrophysical parameters / FLAME masses where quality permits.

## G001H

Completed GFRO hardening:

- [[../Experiments/G001H GFRO-Hardened Gaia Local Ledger]]

G001H declares the retained sources, emits a Node -1 diagnostic registry, audits witness density, mass policy, softening, and branch family, and records certification boundaries.

Remaining block: it is not official Gaia mass-certified and not public-certified.

Falsifier: if scalar and electric-Weyl/eigenframe channels converge together under every reasonable coarse branch and quality cut, the B027-B037 mechanism does not generalize cleanly into the local Gaia field.

## G002

Use Gaia DR3 non-single-star and wide-binary catalogs.

Falsifier: if pair-resolved and center-of-mass ledgers are indistinguishable across separation and mass-ratio bins at Atlas witness resolution, binary structure is not a meaningful next driver of the transfer asymmetry.

## G003

Use ATNF pulsars, McGill magnetars, and BlackCAT/X-ray binary compact-object tags.

Falsifier: if compact overlays do not change local tensor/eigenframe witnesses beyond uncertainty and resolution limits, compact objects should not be used as a public-facing support claim.

## G004

Compare Gaia-only, Gaia-plus-binary, Gaia-plus-compact, and Gaia-plus-all ledgers.

Falsifier: if all augmented ledgers collapse back to the Gaia-only transfer hierarchy, the extreme-source lane is not needed for the modest paper.

## Boundary

Do not say:

- Atlas modeled the whole Milky Way.
- Gaia proves the galaxy rotation claim.
- Compact objects explain galaxy-scale residuals.
- Binaries replace dark matter.
- Full baryonic closure has been achieved.

Allowed if earned:

- Atlas built a real-data stellar-source ledger from Gaia.
- Atlas tested whether curvature-ledger fidelity survives common coarse representations.
- Atlas separated scalar rotation-like summaries from local electric-Weyl/eigenframe structure in a measured stellar field.
- Atlas evaluated whether binary and compact-source structure changes the tensor/scalar transfer hierarchy.
