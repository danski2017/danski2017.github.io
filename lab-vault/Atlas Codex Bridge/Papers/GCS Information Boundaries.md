# GCS Information Boundaries

## Status

Public companion paper. May 2026 draft.

## Digest

Interprets GCS surfaces as modular attribution boundaries for source identity in nested gravitational scenes. In the minimal normalized support model, binary attribution entropy is maximized at the parity condition `Q_A = Q_K`, giving a formal bridge between contribution-context parity and source-attribution uncertainty. It extends the GCS idea toward stress-energy bookkeeping: source-resolved inside, attribution-contested at parity, and context-assimilated outside.

## Current Relevance

Useful for boundary language and information-survival framing.

## Current Use

Connects naturally to the current coarse-graining question: what information survives compression into summaries?

## Claim Boundary

The information-boundary reading is speculative and bookkeeping-focused. It does not alter stress-energy, change Einstein's equation, claim information destruction, introduce new observables, or make GCS surfaces physical membranes. Dynamic worldsheet language is a future extension, not a certified mechanism.
