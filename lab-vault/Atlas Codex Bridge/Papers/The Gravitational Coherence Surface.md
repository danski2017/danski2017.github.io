# The Gravitational Coherence Surface

## Status

Public series foundation paper. April 2026.

## Digest

Introduces the gravitational coherence surface as a parity horizon of gravitational identity: the surface where a declared gravitational contribution reaches equality with its derived gravitational context under a declared readability rule. The paper establishes the four-part root grammar: declared scene, declared contribution, derived context, and equality/readability rule. It explicitly separates GCS readability from causal horizons, event horizons, material membranes, force boundaries, orbital stability limits, and dynamical transitions.

## Current Relevance

Introduces the GCS concept as a relational gravitational readability/coherence structure. The key lesson is that gravitational attribution can be scene-relative without implying discontinuity in the field. The Earth-Moon-Sun example is an early diagnostic warning: dynamical belonging, contribution-context parity, and full nested-scene structure are distinct layers.

## Current Use

Use for public origin story and conceptual genealogy. Do not use early surface language to override later ledger/GFRO claim discipline.

## Claim Boundary

The GCS is diagnostic geometry inside a declared scene. It is not a physical wall, causal limit, stability guarantee, observable by itself, or modification of GR. Later work should preserve the original distinction: the field remains continuous while attribution/readability changes.
