# Mesoscale Gravitational Readability

## Status

Public GCS series paper. April 2026.

## Digest

Frames parity faces, seams, margins, low-margin regions, and transition loci as candidate mesoscale interface geometry between pointwise local field evaluation and coarse-grained or averaged summaries. The paper's central move is descriptive: continuous weak-field systems may have stable, derived comparison structures that are erased when analysis jumps directly from local samples to bulk descriptors.

## Current Relevance

Direct ancestor of the current mesoscale curvature program. It frames the lab's interest in structure between local sources and global summaries.

## Current Use

Use as conceptual bridge from GCS readability to B006-B013 coarse-graining and averaging studies.

## Claim Boundary

Mesoscale readability structures are diagnostic overlays, not membranes, attractors, geodesics, force barriers, physical interfaces, or invariants. The paper proposes a hypothesis to test: stable derived geometry may exist in the under-described middle between local evaluation and coarse summary.
