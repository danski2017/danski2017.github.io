## Core Result

For a uniform-density oblate spheroid, the density-gradient support is concentrated at the body boundary. That allows boundary-supported reconstruction of the interior tidal tensor and its trace-free electric-Weyl proxy.

The paper's working tensor channel is:

```text
T_ij = partial_i partial_j Phi
E_ij = T_ij - (1/3) delta_ij tr(T)
```

The reconstruction validates against the analytic ellipsoid tensor to near machine precision on compact interior points.
