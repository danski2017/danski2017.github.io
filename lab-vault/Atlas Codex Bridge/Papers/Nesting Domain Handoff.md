# Nesting Domain Handoff

## Status

Public GCS series paper. April 27, 2026.

## Digest

Names the nesting domain handoff problem: how a high-resolution child scene is embedded within, supported by, and interpreted through a lower-resolution parent context. The paper argues that scene declaration is mathematically meaningful rather than passive bookkeeping. It introduces a context-calculus vocabulary around source rosters, time slices, readable-field definitions, datum conventions, causal access, parent contexts, child domains, handoff confidence, uncertainty, and tidal-orientation witnesses.

## Current Relevance

Important for source/context and domain handoff thinking. Current finite curvature-budget work also identifies handoff as open rather than solved.

## Current Use

Use for lineage and vocabulary. Keep dynamic handoff claims bounded.

## Claim Boundary

The handoff grammar does not modify local GR equations or claim field discontinuity. It proposes a modular interpretation layer for declared scenes. Treat predictions about tidal eigenstructure, released-lattice deformation, and trajectory diagnostics as research prompts unless backed by later certified runs.
