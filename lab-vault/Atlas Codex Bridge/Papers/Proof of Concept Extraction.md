# Proof of Concept Extraction

## Status

Public GCS series paper. April 2026.

## Digest

Reports proof-of-concept extraction of many-source readability structures under the scalar tidal-readable branch `Q_i ∝ M_i / r_i^3`. It demonstrates three operational classes: recoverable local readable pockets around subordinate sources in dominant environments, dense many-source boundary-support architecture generated by source-readable competition rather than imposed tessellation, and non-degenerate support responses from matched-budget source ensembles under arrangement changes.

## Current Relevance

Shows the extraction lineage of the lab: computational artifact first, interpretation after validation.

## Current Use

Useful for method history. Current extraction standards should be governed by GFRO, scene passports, and ledger-first discipline.

## Claim Boundary

The extraction result is operational recoverability under a declared branch. It is not branch-independent uniqueness, final invariance, direct observability, a new force, a physical boundary, or modified gravity. Rendered supports are downstream presentations of extracted supports, not the source of the claim.
