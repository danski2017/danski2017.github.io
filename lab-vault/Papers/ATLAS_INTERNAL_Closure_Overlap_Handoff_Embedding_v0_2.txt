================================================================================
ABSTRACT
================================================================================

This internal note synchronizes the earlier hierarchical handoff memo with current Atlas vocabulary, architecture doctrine, and claim discipline. The viable core is the method branch:

    closure -> overlap -> handoff -> embedding

The branch tries to convert raw many-source comparison output into hierarchy-aware local transition structure. Closure identifies the meaningful contextual domain under a declared readability rule. Overlap identifies which retained source-local domains coexist and therefore deserve pairwise attention. Handoff identifies local identity-transition supports inside overlap-valid regions. Embedding attaches hierarchy and parent-context tags to those handoff supports.

This branch remains private and internal because it governs extraction logic, overlap gating, hierarchy tagging, and implementation-sensitive diagnostics. It does not modify general relativity, introduce a new force, define a new physical object, or replace canonical Phase I parity/GCS extraction. It may become valuable only if it improves relevance filtering, reduces pairwise clutter, stabilizes handoff structures, and passes repeatability and sensitivity checks.

5. CORE HYPOTHESIS
================================================================================

Internal working claim:
In many-source weak-field scenes, handoff structure becomes more meaningful when extracted only inside regions where retained source-local closure domains overlap. Closure defines where a source or retained cell has contextual relevance. Overlap defines which competitors coexist in a meaningful domain. Handoff marks local transitions in source-readable dominance inside those overlap-valid regions. Embedding tags describe how those transitions sit inside larger closure or parent-context structure.

Compact statement:
Closure determines meaningful domain. Overlap determines relevance. Handoff determines local identity transition. Embedding records hierarchy context.

This is a method hypothesis, not a new physical claim.

6. FORMAL SETUP
================================================================================

Let a declared weak-field scene contain physical sources S_i with masses M_i and positions x_i. Let x be a sampled point on a declared slice Sigma_t.

The Phase I scalar readable branch is

    Q_i(x) = M_i / (||x - x_i||^2 + eps^2)^(3/2),

or, away from sources in analytic shorthand,

    Q_i(x) proportional to M_i / r_i(x)^3.

Pairwise residuals are

    Psi_ij(x) = Q_i(x) - Q_j(x).

A source-readable margin against the strongest local competitor may be defined as

    m_i(x) = Q_i(x) - max_{j != i} Q_j(x).

A retained closure domain for source or cell i may be represented generically as

    C_i = { x : source or cell i passes the declared closure/context rule at x }.

The exact closure rule must be declared per branch. Examples may include positive readable margin, comparison to a derived context, comparison to a declared parent-context readability baseline, or retained GCS/parity-context membership.

For two retained domains C_i and C_j, define overlap support

    O_ij = C_i intersect C_j.

The pair (i,j) is overlap-active if O_ij is nonempty and passes declared size, density, stability, or support thresholds.

A handoff support H_ij is a retained local transition structure inside O_ij where source-readable identity changes between i and j, or where their declared dominance/ordering is locally unresolved under the declared rule.

Symbolically:

    H_ij subset O_ij,

with candidate detection based on one or more of:

    Psi_ij(x) near 0,
    sign change of Psi_ij across neighboring samples,
    winner identity change across neighboring samples,
    low margin between top readable competitors,
    stable local transition across sampling and tolerance sweeps.

================================================================================
7. PIPELINE
================================================================================

The synchronized branch pipeline is:

1. Ledger / relational state.
   Compute and store Q_i, Psi_ij, source rankings, margins, low-margin flags, and relevant sampling metadata in the certified relational ledger.

2. Closure.
   Extract or identify retained source-local or cell-local closure domains under the declared readability and parent-context rule.

3. Overlap.
   Build an overlap graph whose nodes are retained domains and whose edges indicate overlap-active domain pairs.

4. Handoff.
   Within overlap-active regions, extract local identity-transition supports using declared residual, margin, winner-change, or low-margin criteria.

5. Embedding.
   Tag handoff supports with hierarchy context: enclosing closure domains, parent-context relation, depth proxy, dominant contextual actor, overlap strength, and local compression state.

6. Validation.
   Test whether extracted handoff supports persist under tolerance, sampling, domain, and nearby-scene perturbations.

7. Rendering.
   Render closure domains, handoff supports, and embedding tags as downstream visualizations of ledgered diagnostics.

================================================================================
8. INTERPRETATION
================================================================================

Closure domains provide contextual enclosure or local relevance structure. They do not become physical shells or force walls.

Overlap-active edges identify which local domains coexist meaningfully at the declared scale and tolerance. They are a relevance-filtering device.

Handoff supports describe local identity-transition structure inside overlap-valid regions. They are not geodesics, attractors, physical barriers, or direct observables.

Embedding tags give provisional hierarchy context. They are not automatic parent-scene assignments and do not override Node 1 declarations.

The branch is valuable only if it reduces arbitrary pairwise clutter, improves junction legibility, clarifies source-in-system interpretation, or supports repeatable hierarchy-aware extraction.

11. TESTABLE CONSEQUENCES
================================================================================

T1. Relevance gain.
Overlap-gated handoff should produce fewer irrelevant pair structures than naive all-pairs parity extraction.

T2. Stability gain.
Handoff supports inside overlap-valid regions should be more stable under modest perturbations than unfiltered pairwise candidate surfaces.

T3. Junction clarity.
Triple and higher-order negotiation regions should become clearer when interpreted through overlap and local ordering rather than as accidental intersections of unrelated pair sheets.

T4. Hierarchy clarity.
Embedding tags should clarify whether a handoff support sits inside one closure domain, multiple overlapping closure domains, or a broader parent-context region.

T5. Compression clarity.
Handoff density and low-margin counts should correlate with multi-source compression rather than with sampling artifacts.

T6. Render-to-ledger agreement.
The visual handoff skeleton should correspond to machine-readable handoff components, overlap edges, and closure records.

================================================================================
12. FAILURE MODES
================================================================================

F1. Handoff extraction adds no information beyond existing retained pairwise parity faces.

F2. Overlap gating discards meaningful structures or keeps arbitrary ones.

F3. Handoff supports teleport under modest threshold, sampling, domain, or source-position changes.

F4. Embedding-depth tags are unstable or merely visual.

F5. The branch depends on a scalar parent-context baseline that is not lawfully declared.

F6. Renders look convincing but ledger diagnostics do not support the claimed structure.

F7. The pipeline becomes more complex without improving canonical Phase I reliability.

If these failure modes dominate, the branch should remain historical or scratch-only.
