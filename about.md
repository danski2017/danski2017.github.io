# About

Relational Labs is the public research home for the Atlas Solver platform, a weak-field multi-source GR computational geometry laboratory.

## **Lead Researcher**

Daniel Scieszinski is an independent researcher working in geometric methods in General Relativity, weak-field curvature structure, and multi-body spacetime simulation. Alongside this research, Daniel works professionally in IT and insurance analysis and is also an artist and author. His work often focuses on long-horizon projects that combine technical systems, geometry, and creative structure.

## AI Research Team

Relational Labs operates with a multi-wing AI research architecture as a core part of its methodology. Claude (Anthropic) serves as the primary research collaborator for independent reproduction, synthesis, and drafting — providing a second analytical voice separate from the lead researcher's own reasoning. GPT (OpenAI) handles doctrinal authority, canonical vocabulary, and formula verification against established literature. Claude Code (Anthropic) runs the original experimental computation, executing simulations and diagnostic pipelines directly. Codex (OpenAI) serves as lead lab technician, managing computational infrastructure and execution workflows. Gemini (Google) supports publication preparation and web presence. This structure is not cosmetic: each system is assigned to tasks that match its operational strengths, and results are cross-checked across wings before conclusions are drawn.
