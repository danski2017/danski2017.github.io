# **Interactive Field Simulations**

*The following experiments provide a visual interface for the mathematical structures defined in the Atlas research papers. These sandboxes utilize Three.js to render real-time gravitational tensors and jurisdiction boundaries.*

<br>

---

## **Experiment I - Local Stellar Neighborhood - N Parity Stack 2×3 Matrix**
*An empirical campaign visualizing 57 local stellar sources. This matrix explores source-source pairwise foam versus source-context sovereignty cores across three tiers of potential support and acceleration magnitude.*
